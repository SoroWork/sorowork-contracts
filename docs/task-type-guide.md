# Community Work Task Type Guide

Best practices for structuring tasks on SoroWork by category.

## Task Categories

### Cleanup Tasks
- **Description template**: "Remove [debris type] from [location]. Area is approximately [size]."
- **Evidence required**: Before and after photos from the same angle
- **Typical reward**: 20–100 USDC depending on area size
- **Example**: "Clean up litter and debris from Riverside Park main trail (0.5 mile stretch)"

### Repair Tasks
- **Description template**: "Repair [item] at [location]. Current condition: [description]."
- **Evidence required**: Before photo, in-progress photo, completed repair photo
- **Typical reward**: 50–300 USDC depending on complexity
- **Example**: "Fill pothole on Oak Street near intersection with Main (approx 2ft diameter)"

### Landscaping Tasks
- **Description template**: "Perform [landscaping work] at [location]. Tools needed: [list]."
- **Evidence required**: Before and after photos, wide shot showing full area
- **Typical reward**: 30–200 USDC
- **Example**: "Mow and edge the community garden perimeter at Cedar Park"

### Painting Tasks
- **Description template**: "Paint [surface] at [location] using [color/type]. Surface area: [estimate]."
- **Evidence required**: Before, during, and after photos
- **Typical reward**: 50–250 USDC
- **Example**: "Repaint park bench set (3 benches) at Memorial Square — forest green"

### Construction Tasks
- **Description template**: "Build/install [item] at [location]. Materials [provided/needed]."
- **Evidence required**: Progress photos at key stages, final completion photo
- **Typical reward**: 100–500 USDC
- **Example**: "Install wheelchair ramp at community center side entrance"

## Writing Good Task Descriptions

1. Be specific about the location (address or landmark)
2. Describe the current condition and desired outcome
3. List any tools or materials the worker needs to bring
4. Set a realistic timeline
5. Specify the evidence format you expect
